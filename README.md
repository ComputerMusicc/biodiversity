### Data
1. Name of Data Source: Conservation Statuses of Species
1. Owner of Data: National Parks Service
1. The data contains...
	a. 'observations.csv': recorded sightings of different species from multiple national parks over 7 days. A table with columns 
		1. scientific_name
		1. park_name 
		1. observations
	b. 'species_info.csv': details about these species and their conservation status. A table with columns
		1. Category 
		1. scientific_name
		1. common_names
		1. conservation_status
